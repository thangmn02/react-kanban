# Gymnopédie: contours exist, but MELODIA rejects them

All four investigated stem locations contain extractable pitch contours. With only `guessUnvoiced=true`, the installed MELODIA emits pitches with **negative confidence in all 51 inspected frames at each location**. These are rejected/speculative contours, not recovered melody notes or approved events. Total loss of pitch evidence is not supported by this diagnostic.

The exposed installed spectral, salience, contour and melody components reproduce both saved baseline pitch/confidence arrays **exactly**. Original/stem inputs retain the same ten-second context, equal-loudness preprocessing, 44,100 Hz rate, frame 2048 and hop 128. One condition was tested on both inputs; only the unvoiced-estimation flag changed. Segmentation was not invoked for the guessed output.

Salience values are signal-dependent contour statistics, not calibrated note probabilities. Spectral examples are from aligned frames approximately 55 ms after each diagnostic location; they are not averages over the entire phrase. No insufficiency conclusion is based on event counts. These waveform peaks are not ground truth.

The [Essentia contour-selection source](https://github.com/MTG/essentia/blob/master/src/algorithms/tonal/pitchcontoursmelody.cpp) applies relative contour salience/voicing selection before octave/outlier filtering. Its guessed fallback can expose ignored contours with negative confidence. This explains why negative confidence establishes rejection but alone cannot identify which filtering stage rejected a contour. The [API documents that sign convention](https://essentia.upf.edu/reference/std_PitchContoursMelody.html). Source-derived gate statistics are diagnostic only; no thresholds were adjusted.

## Unresolved questions — stop here

Do the surviving contours correspond to the listener's primary melody, especially the competing roots at 0.360 s and the sustained contour crossing 1.138 s? Does the stem audibly alter those notes, or remove accompaniment? At 5.074 s, the later filtering mechanism is strongly indicated, but its full internal rejection state is not exposed. No speculative pitch should be integrated on this evidence, and no further experiment starts automatically.
