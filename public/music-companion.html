KORA MUSIC COMPANION

Your music, beside your focus timer.

Connect browser music to the Kora web dock or Windows app. Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave.

Early access: not in the browser store yet, so it takes four steps to install.

  1. 1

    Download and unzip

    Download the file, then right-click it in Downloads and choose “Extract All” (on Mac, double-click it). Keep the folder.

    Download music extension
  2. 2

    Open the Extensions page

    Copy this address into your browser’s address bar and press Enter.

    chrome://extensions
  3. 3

    Load the folder

    Turn on “Developer mode”, click “Load unpacked” and choose the folder you unzipped.

    Developer mode Load unpacked
  4. 4

    Play music and open your dock

    Web: refresh Kora and open Floating Focus.

    Windows app: open dock settings → Install or update Companion, then load the folder it shows.

    Return to Kora

No song showing up?

Press play in the music tab once. Keep Kora and the music in the same browser and profile, then refresh Kora. Turn off duplicate copies of the Companion.

Update the Companion

Windows app: dock settings → Install or update Companion. Web: download again, replace the files in your extension folder, click Reload on the Extensions page, then refresh Kora.

How the beat grid works

Kick, Snare, Hat and Bass light up from audio analyzed in your browser. If the grid stays gray, click the Kora icon on the music tab. Busy songs can cause missed or extra flashes, and other sound in the same browser (like calls) can affect them.

Audio is analyzed on your device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
