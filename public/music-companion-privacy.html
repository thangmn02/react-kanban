Music Companion privacy

The extension connects supported browser music to Kanban Floating Focus for playback controls and beat lighting.

What it reads

While Floating Focus is open, it reads title, artist, playback position/state and site name from supported YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify Web tabs. Localhost access supports development. It does not read account passwords, messages or browsing history.

Optional live beat sync

Clicking Kanban Music Companion while viewing your selected music tab allows Chrome to capture that tab for local beat analysis. Chrome shows a capture indicator, and the dock displays a listening message. The capture recipe initially requests audio and video; video tracks are stopped immediately and are never analyzed, displayed or recorded. Audio is analyzed locally and passed to your speakers so playback continues. No microphone access is requested.

Where data goes

Metadata and detected band onsets go only to the Kanban app tab at kanthangboard.netlify.app or approved local development origins. Audio and video are never recorded, stored or uploaded. There is no tracking service, listening history, music login or API key.

Permissions

Site access and scripting read media state and invoke play/pause. The music clock script runs on supported music sites; the app relay runs on Kanban. activeTab and tabCapture support capture after extension invocation. An offscreen USER_MEDIA document hosts the temporary local audio analysis.

Your control

Pause the song, close its tab, or close Floating Focus to stop capture. A short lease also releases capture if the app disappears unexpectedly. Failed capture quietly returns to song-clock lighting. Disable or remove the extension at any time. It does not control native desktop music apps or other browser profiles.
