Beat grid stays gray?

Click the Kora icon on the music tab to allow live beats. A new music tab or an extension reload may need one more click.

Audio is analyzed only in your browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Music not appearing?

Keep Kora and the music tab open in this same browser and profile. Install the Companion in each browser profile you use.
